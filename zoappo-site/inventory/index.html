    All-in-one order, stock, purchase, sales and warehouse management

    With real-time tracking, automated workflows, and easy integration with other Zoho apps, Zoho Inventory streamlines operations across multiple locations and keeps every transaction accurate.

    Stock Management

    Track inventory levels in real time, manage multiple warehouses, automate stock updates, and prevent overstocking or stockouts.

    Order Fulfillment

    Automate order processing, track sales and purchase orders, manage shipping, and update inventory levels for timely deliveries.

    Inventory Reports

    Stock levels, sales trends, purchase history, and order statuses to monitor performance and make data-driven decisions.
